# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation respectful, constructive and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing technical disagreements.
- Assume good intent while evaluating evidence and behavior independently.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Respect service terms, privacy and applicable law.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized language and attention
- Trolling, personal attacks or repeated bad-faith disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, tokens, account exports or private logs
- Encouraging abuse, unauthorized access or destructive activity
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile contact method; do not open a public issue containing sensitive information.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as reasonably possible. Maintainers must protect the privacy and safety of reporters.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where someone represents the project.
